Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

1. Introduction

At Velour Pulse, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Quiz Responses

When you take our archetype quiz, we collect your answers to generate personalized results. Your quiz data is not sold or shared without your consent.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies to remember your preferences and basic analytics to understand usage patterns. We do not use invasive tracking.

Affiliate Links

We use affiliate programs (We-Vibe, Dame Products, etc.). When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set cookies. We do not receive your personal information from these retailers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver quiz results and personalized recommendations
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To comply with age verification (18+)
  • To generate affiliate revenue

4. Affiliate Disclosure

Velour Pulse participates in affiliate programs. We may earn commissions when you purchase through our links. All affiliate links are clearly marked. This does not cost you extra.

5. Data Security

Your quiz data is stored on secure servers with encryption. We use HTTPS to protect data transmission.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your quiz data
  • Request deletion of your responses
  • Clear cookies anytime via browser settings

Contact: privacy@velourpulse.com

7. Age Requirements

Velour Pulse is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
