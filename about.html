About Velour Pulse

Our Mission

Velour Pulse exists to create safe, judgment-free space for sexual exploration and self-discovery.

Why We Built This

We noticed something: people want to explore their sexuality, but they don't know where to start. There's shame around desire. So we built Velour Pulse as the platform we wished existed.

Our Values

🔒 Privacy

Your data is yours. No judgment. No selling.

✨ Authenticity

All forms of sexual expression are valid.

📚 Education

Honest, evidence-based content that empowers.

🤝 Community

Safe space to explore together.
